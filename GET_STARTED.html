Atlas Lab — Online 3D Anatomy

No Python installation is needed by visitors. Once this website is published, just open its address in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox with WebGL enabled.

Dissect the body

  1. Open the lab. The first anatomical layer is the skeleton.
  2. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  3. Click a real anatomical mesh; select Hide, Isolate, Transparent, or Focus.
  4. Enable muscles, nerves, organs, or other systems from the right.
  5. Use Guided Lessons or Practice Exam for structured study.

Publish your own website

The package includes a .github/workflows/deploy.yml action that fetches the anatomical meshes into your website at deployment. Read DEPLOY_ONLINE.md for step-by-step hosting guidance. Models are not physically included in the initial ZIP.

Accuracy and terms

Model identities come from the open Z-Anatomy-derived atlas; short study cards were cross-checked against textbook topics, but no clinician has independently validated every entry. See accuracy review and license and attribution. Educational use only; not for clinical practice.
