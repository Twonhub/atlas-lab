TRANSPARENCY & EVIDENCE

Medical accuracy and model provenance

What has been checked, what has not, and which licensing obligations accompany these models.

Medical review status

Mesh labels: imported verbatim from Z-Anatomy-derived source data. Some names are abbreviations, aliases, or nonstandard taxonomy. They have not been audited one by one.

Study cards: manually cross-checked against cited OpenStax chapters or topics and checked for missing data, duplicates, and question inconsistencies. Not independently checked by an anatomist.

3D positioning: inherited from source model; not independently validated for every nerve, muscle attachment, or variation.

Clinical: not a diagnostic, surgical, or patient-care tool.
