## Medical content limitations
3D mesh names and positions were imported from a source anatomical dataset, not independently validated one by one. Study summaries are educational and require cross-checking with an anatomy textbook. Not for patient care or diagnosis. [OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e).
